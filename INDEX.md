# Technical insight knowledge index

## Key takeaway

Use the catalogue to find a definition explanation representation operation or Practice.

## Summary

This edition contains 51 core knowledge objects and 11 supporting Reference records. Each file includes its own local meaning and limits. The catalogue is navigation infrastructure, not another knowledge type.

## Catalogue

### Concepts

- [Technical insight](concepts/technical-insight.md) — An insight is a scoped explanatory understanding that changes what you can predict, investigate or decide.
- [Observation and interpretation](concepts/observation-and-interpretation.md) — Record the event and how it was obtained separately from the explanation you give it.
- [Hypothesis](concepts/hypothesis.md) — A hypothesis is a candidate account with consequences that can be compared with evidence.
- [Mechanism](concepts/mechanism.md) — A mechanism explains the intermediate process connecting a condition or intervention to an outcome.
- [Evidence](concepts/evidence.md) — Evidence is a contribution to a particular claim, not a synonym for a citation or an attractive result.
- [Assumptions and boundary conditions](concepts/assumptions-and-boundary-conditions.md) — An explanation is conditional on what was assumed and on the context in which its evidence applies.
- [System boundary](concepts/system-boundary.md) — A boundary determines what is treated as internal, external, observed and controllable.
- [Measurement uncertainty](concepts/measurement-uncertainty.md) — A reported value needs its measurement process and uncertainty context to support a technical decision.
- [Comparison and control](concepts/comparison-and-control.md) — A comparison is informative only about differences it can separate from competing influences.
- [Prediction](concepts/prediction.md) — A prediction states an expected observable consequence before examining the result used to assess it.
- [Tradeoff](concepts/tradeoff.md) — A design preference depends on objectives and constraints as well as on technical facts.
- [Transfer](concepts/transfer.md) — Reusing an insight requires checking the new conditions, not copying the old conclusion unchanged.
- [Provenance](concepts/provenance.md) — Provenance identifies how a result was produced; it helps inspection but does not certify correctness.

### Principles

- [Measurements include the observation process](principles/measurements-include-the-observation-process.md) — A reading depends on the target and on the instrument, sampling and processing used to observe it.
- [Associations do not determine intervention effects](principles/associations-do-not-determine-interventions.md) — A relationship in observed data alone does not identify what changing one factor will cause.
- [Explanations can share the same predictions](principles/explanations-can-share-the-same-predictions.md) — Evidence separates explanations only where their predicted consequences differ sufficiently.
- [Factor effects can depend on other factors](principles/factor-effects-can-depend-on-other-factors.md) — An adjustment can help under one setting and hinder under another.
- [Fit does not establish model validity](principles/fit-does-not-establish-model-validity.md) — Matching data is one check of a model, not proof of correct implementation, mechanism or future applicability.
- [Validation has a domain of applicability](principles/validation-has-a-domain-of-applicability.md) — Evidence supports a model or method for specified contexts and uses, not for every future application.
- [Repeated reports can share one evidence base](principles/repeated-reports-can-share-one-evidence-base.md) — Counting documents can overstate the number of independent observations supporting a claim.
- [Reproducibility does not establish correctness](principles/reproducibility-does-not-establish-correctness.md) — The same inputs and procedure can reproduce the same error.
- [Decision preference depends on objectives and uncertainty](principles/decision-preference-depends-on-objectives-and-uncertainty.md) — The same engineering facts can support different actions when objectives, constraints or uncertainty change.
- [A useful intervention need not identify its mechanism](principles/a-useful-intervention-need-not-identify-its-mechanism.md) — Evidence that a change helps and evidence explaining why it helps are different claims.
- [Novelty and evidential support are different properties](principles/novelty-and-support-are-different-properties.md) — An unfamiliar explanation can be well established elsewhere, and a new explanation can remain unsupported.

### Models

- [Question and system boundary model](models/question-and-system-boundary-model.md) — A useful investigation connects a decision to an observable question inside an explicit boundary.
- [Claim evidence and inference model](models/claim-evidence-inference-model.md) — An evidence link is meaningful only with the inference and scope that connect it to a claim.
- [Hypothesis prediction matrix](models/hypothesis-prediction-matrix.md) — Choose observations where credible rival explanations predict meaningfully different outcomes.
- [Measurement and uncertainty model](models/measurement-and-uncertainty-model.md) — Model the route from the target quantity to the reported value before interpreting a small difference.
- [Causal path and intervention model](models/causal-path-and-intervention-model.md) — An intervention changes a system relation; a correlation only describes observed co-variation.
- [Model validity envelope](models/model-validity-envelope.md) — Record where a model was checked and what decision those checks can support.
- [Decision and transfer model](models/decision-and-transfer-model.md) — Apply an insight by joining scoped evidence to a new context and a real decision.
- [Insight learning cycle](models/insight-learning-cycle.md) — Discovery, validation and application are linked activities, not a one-way proof pipeline.

### Methods

- [Frame an insight question](methods/frame-an-insight-question.md) — Turn a broad technical concern into a scoped uncertainty that matters to a decision.
- [Map a technical landscape](methods/map-a-technical-landscape.md) — Explore mechanisms and alternatives around a goal before treating the first plausible solution as the only one.
- [Audit an observation](methods/audit-an-observation.md) — Check what was actually measured before explaining why the system behaved that way.
- [Synthesize claim relevant sources](methods/synthesize-claim-relevant-sources.md) — Compare what sources establish for a claim rather than counting how many documents mention it.
- [Generate competing explanations](methods/generate-competing-explanations.md) — Construct a small set of plausible accounts that differ in what they predict.
- [Build an explanatory model](methods/build-an-explanatory-model.md) — Represent the smallest set of relationships needed to explain or predict the decision-relevant behavior.
- [Design a discriminating test](methods/design-a-discriminating-test.md) — Select a permitted comparison where rival accounts differ beyond relevant uncertainty.
- [Execute a bounded test](methods/execute-a-bounded-test.md) — Execute the approved comparison and preserve what happened, including departures from the plan.
- [Evaluate results and uncertainty](methods/evaluate-results-and-uncertainty.md) — Judge the claim against what actually happened, its uncertainty and competing explanations.
- [Challenge and replicate an explanation](methods/challenge-and-replicate-an-explanation.md) — Choose a challenge that changes an evidential dependency instead of merely rerunning the same calculation.
- [Compare technical alternatives](methods/compare-technical-alternatives.md) — Separate evidence about consequences from preferences about which consequences are desirable.
- [Assess transfer to a new context](methods/assess-transfer-to-a-new-context.md) — Compare source and target assumptions before adopting the same action.
- [Write an insight record](methods/write-an-insight-record.md) — Record the explanation, evidence, limits and consequences without turning a proposal into an observed result.
- [Review and revise knowledge](methods/review-and-revise-knowledge.md) — Revise the claim and affected uses when new evidence changes its support or scope.

### Practices

- [Discover and validate a technical insight](practices/discover-and-validate-a-technical-insight.md) — Build a scoped explanation by connecting a consequential question to evidence that can challenge competing accounts.
- [Investigate a technical opportunity](practices/investigate-a-technical-opportunity.md) — Discover useful possibilities by connecting a capability gap to mechanisms, alternatives and decisive evidence.
- [Assess a technical claim](practices/assess-a-technical-claim.md) — Evaluate the exact claim and its supporting chain before adopting the conclusion.
- [Apply and transfer an insight](practices/apply-and-transfer-an-insight.md) — Use an insight through a bounded decision and a target-context check rather than copying its original prescription.
- [Maintain technical insight knowledge](practices/maintain-technical-insight-knowledge.md) — Keep the knowledge and its uses consistent when evidence, context or interpretation changes.
