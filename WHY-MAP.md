## The evidence to action boundary

A Method produces an output appropriate to its task. A diagram of causes supplies candidates, a chart supplies a view, an experiment supplies observations under a design, and a decision matrix supplies a preference-dependent comparison. The [Practices](INTEGRATED-PRACTICES.md) combine these without silently strengthening their evidential status. [Principles](INDEX.md#principle-files) retain explanatory relationships rather than commands.
