# Horizon scanning

## Key takeaway

Collect and interpret dated signals of possible change relevant to a defined decision horizon.

## Summary

Collect and interpret dated signals of possible change relevant to a defined decision horizon. Use it for the stated output, not as a substitute for evidence or an entire engineering workflow.

## Established basis

**Technique:** Horizon scanning. **Role:** analytical technique. The named technique is reused from [The Futures Toolkit](../references/government-office-for-science-2024-futures-toolkit.md). The worksheet, engineering example and local safeguards below are our exposition and application; they are not a new method, an official standard implementation or evidence that this workflow is effective.

## Inputs and prerequisites

A purpose, decision owner, time horizon, search boundary and sources spanning more than publicity. Work with public or authorized information only.

## Rationale

Scanning outside a favored technical category can reveal emerging alternatives and external conditions that a narrow implementation search misses.

## Procedure

1. Define the decision, useful capability, time horizon and categories of change; allow new categories to emerge.
2. Search diverse sources, recording query, date, coverage and the original evidence behind announcements.
3. Capture each signal with what changed, why it might matter, maturity claims, uncertainty and related reports.
4. Group related signals and inspect counter-signals or missing perspectives. Distinguish multiple reports from independent developments.
5. Choose signals for deeper investigation, continued monitoring or exclusion, with reasons and a review trigger.

## Worked example

**Synthetic example, not a measured investigation.** Several synthetic announcements mention low-power sensing, but all quote the same laboratory demo. A contrary signal concerns unreliable operation under changing temperature. The output is a lead for a contextual test, not a trend forecast.

## Working template

| Signal and date | Original source | Evidence family | Possible implication | Counter-signal | Coverage limit | Next decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| What changed | Locator | Shared or independent | Conditional effect | Challenge | Omitted scope | Study / monitor / stop |

## Output and validation

Deliver a dated signal register and shortlist with uncertainty. Check whether a different source set would likely change the shortlist.

## Limits and optional tools

Frequency of mention is not importance, truth or probability. Missing search hits do not prove absence. This use follows foresight scanning, not an automatic predictor of winning technologies.

Paper or Markdown is sufficient unless the calculation or experiment requires more. A spreadsheet or established analysis package is optional; AI may suggest candidates but does not supply verified evidence, measurements or permissions. The downstream Practice selects and combines this technique; it need not be used in every investigation.
