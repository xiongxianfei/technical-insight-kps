# Compare technical alternatives

## Key takeaway

Separate evidence about consequences from preferences about which consequences are desirable.

## Summary

Use after the technical question is sufficiently bounded to compare actions. Include no change and a simpler alternative when credible. The output is an explicit choice or unresolved decision, not a universal best solution.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Decision owner, purpose, constraints, alternatives, scoped evidence, costs and uncertainty. Mandatory safeguards are screened before preference scoring.

## Rationale

The same insight can justify several Methods under different goals. A choice needs an explicit bridge from evidence to objectives and acceptable risk.

## Procedure

1. State the decision, authority and time horizon; separate hard constraints from preferences.
2. List credible alternatives including no change and a lower-complexity option.
3. For each alternative summarize mechanism, evidence applicability, benefit, harm, cost, reversibility and major unknowns.
4. Compare alternatives under the same context and outcome definitions. Avoid presenting incomparable benchmarks as one ranking.
5. Test sensitivity to uncertain premises and weights. Identify what additional evidence could reverse the choice.
6. Select, defer or reject with reasons. Record monitoring signals and who may stop or revise implementation.
7. Keep the design choice in a Method/Practice or decision record, not as an imperative Principle.

## Expected effect

A decision whose underlying facts, preferences and uncertainties are separable.

## Validation and counterevidence

A changed duty cycle or outcome weight may legitimately change the preferred thermal option. If the recommendation stays fixed regardless of purpose, inspect whether it is a preconceived technology preference.

## Limits

No aggregate score cancels a safety violation. This is not legal, financial or certification advice and does not replace required project approvals.
