# Technology roadmapping

## Key takeaway

Connect needs, capabilities and technological resources over time through conditional milestones.

## Summary

Connect needs, capabilities and technological resources over time through conditional milestones. Use it for the stated output, not as a substitute for evidence or an entire engineering workflow.

## Established basis

**Technique:** Technology roadmapping. **Role:** analytical technique. The named technique is reused from [Technology Roadmapping](../references/ifm-engage-undated-technology-roadmapping.md); [The Futures Toolkit](../references/government-office-for-science-2024-futures-toolkit.md). The worksheet, engineering example and local safeguards below are our exposition and application; they are not a new method, an official standard implementation or evidence that this workflow is effective.

## Inputs and prerequisites

A strategic or engineering need, candidate capabilities and technologies, evidence gaps, decision owners and a useful planning horizon.

## Rationale

A technology list does not show how capabilities become possible. Dependencies and evidence gates make the development assumptions visible and revisable.

## Procedure

1. State the objective and time horizon; distinguish desired dates from evidence-supported estimates.
2. Lay out needs, product or system capabilities, technologies and enabling resources as linked layers.
3. Identify dependencies and alternative routes. Include critical demonstrations, integration and operating-context evidence.
4. For each milestone record evidence required, owner, resource assumption and continue, redesign or stop conditions.
5. Challenge the roadmap under plausible scenarios and update it when evidence, needs or constraints change.

## Worked example

**Synthetic example, not a measured investigation.** For a hypothetical monitor: establish event definition -> compare architectures on shared traces -> integrate a prototype -> obtain authorized representative evidence. Failure of detection quality can send the work back to sensing rather than force the next calendar milestone.

## Working template

| Need | Capability | Technology or resource | Dependency | Evidence gate | Owner | Timing basis | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desired outcome | Required ability | Candidate enabler | Necessary predecessor | Observable check | Accountable person | Estimate or target | Redesign or stop |

## Output and validation

Deliver a conditional plan whose links explain why work occurs in the proposed order. Review unsupported dates, missing integration evidence and a credible alternative scenario.

## Limits and optional tools

A roadmap is not a prediction or a promise of technical success. The public IfM overview supports the technique family, not a complete reproduction of its facilitation materials. Stage gates are application choices, not universal TRL requirements.

Paper or Markdown is sufficient unless the calculation or experiment requires more. A spreadsheet or established analysis package is optional; AI may suggest candidates but does not supply verified evidence, measurements or permissions. The downstream Practice selects and combines this technique; it need not be used in every investigation.
