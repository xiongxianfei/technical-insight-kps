# Safety authority and evidence boundaries

## Key takeaway

A knowledge workflow does not authorize an experiment or replace engineering competence.

## Summary

Technical Insight KPS organizes reasoning across domains. Concrete actions still require the right expertise, permissions, measurement capability and safeguards. Stop at those boundaries rather than treating a filled template as approval.

## Before any intervention

Establish who owns the system and data, which changes are permitted, the consequences of error, the required safety/change review, stop conditions and rollback responsibility. Physical laboratories, production services, controlled systems and human studies may impose different mandatory processes. This package does not replace them.

Use existing evidence, software fixtures, analytic checks or simulation when they can answer the question without unnecessary intervention. A simulation has model assumptions; it is not physical validation. A reversible code edit is not necessarily operationally safe.

## Prohibited inference shortcuts

A plausible mechanism is not an observed result. A successful intervention is not complete mechanism proof. A high fit score is not validation for every use. A repeat of the same dataset is not an independent experiment. A new-to-you idea is not necessarily new to the field.

## Confidentiality and records

Do not publish secrets, private measurements, personal information, customer data or restricted documents. Store raw records outside the public repository where appropriate. Public examples here are synthetic and labelled. Blank records are templates, not completed evidence.

## Expertise and stopping

Do not improvise hazardous, medical, security-offensive or safety-critical protocols from generic stages. Stop for unauthorized access, unknown hazards, failed measurement safeguards or unexpected adverse outcomes. Seek the appropriate qualified review. An unresolved result is an acceptable outcome when a safe discriminating test is unavailable.

## What checks establish

Publication checks detect structure, broken links, stale summary dependencies and payload changes. Synthetic example tests establish arithmetic and set behavior under stated assumptions. Neither certifies safety, correctness of all scientific claims, practical effectiveness or originality.
