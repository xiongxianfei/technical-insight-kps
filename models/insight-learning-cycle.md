# Insight learning cycle

## Key takeaway

Discovery, validation and application are linked activities, not a one-way proof pipeline.

## Summary

The cycle turns technical questions into revisable understanding and decision support. It allows observation-led and opportunity-led entry, while preserving evidence and inference as different parts of the argument.

## Representation

```text
Question or opportunity
   -> observations and source needs
   -> rival explanations and models
   -> discriminating test or analysis
   -> evidence evaluation
   -> scoped insight record
   -> application or transfer
   -> outcome and new questions
```

A failed test may return to measurement, assumptions or execution. A result can justify stopping at “unresolved” rather than fabricating a complete explanation.

## Relationships and use

Methods perform bounded operations; Practices orchestrate them for a goal. A technical insight record is supporting experience or a synthesis artifact, not a sixth knowledge type. Reusable definitions, explanations, models and operations are promoted into the appropriate five folders only when independent reuse justifies it.

## Assumptions

The people involved have appropriate expertise and authority. The cycle is an authored workflow whose effectiveness is unmeasured. Provisional records remain distinct from source facts and adopted operating instructions.

## Limits and counterchecks

No amount of structural completeness supplies missing evidence. The cycle should shrink for a trivial issue and deepen with consequence and uncertainty. Novelty, confidence and deployment approval remain independent judgments.
