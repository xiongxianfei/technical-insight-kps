# NIST experimental design guidance

## Key takeaway

Use this source for the located contribution, not for claims it did not evaluate.

## Summary

Design choice depends on the investigation objective. Blocking groups comparable runs around nuisance factors; factorial combinations expose effects that depend on other factors. Repeated observations and experimental replication are not interchangeable. The reference identifies the evidence contribution and access limits; it is not a substitute for reviewing the original when the decision requires it.
