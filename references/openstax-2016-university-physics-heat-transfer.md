# OpenStax heat capacity and heat transfer

## Key takeaway

Use this source for the located contribution, not for claims it did not evaluate.

## Summary

Heat storage and heat transfer describe different quantities. For a constant-heat-capacity approximation without phase change, energy storage is proportional to temperature change. The reference identifies the evidence contribution and access limits; it is not a substitute for reviewing the original when the decision requires it.
