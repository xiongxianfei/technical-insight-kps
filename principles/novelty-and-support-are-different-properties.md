# Novelty and evidential support are different properties

## Key takeaway

An unfamiliar explanation can be well established elsewhere, and a new explanation can remain unsupported.

## Summary

Novelty is relative to a person, community or documented search scope. Evidential support concerns the reason to believe a claim. Neither implies the other.

## Statement

An unfamiliar explanation can be well established elsewhere, and a new explanation can remain unsupported.

## Explanation and derivation

This is an authored conceptual distinction, not an empirical creativity claim. Source provenance and deliberate landscape comparison allow the two judgments to be documented separately.

## Application example

Discovering the transient/steady-state distinction may be a substantial insight for a project even though heat-capacity theory is established. A proposed new thermal mechanism is not credible merely because a search found no identical wording.

## Scope and counterevidence

A bounded literature search cannot certify worldwide originality or patentability. Do not label a known principle as a new scientific discovery. The framework supports useful understanding without requiring novelty.
